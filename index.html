What is Kids Coding?

Coding Is a Superpower — Every Kid Can Learn It

Kids coding means teaching children to give computers step-by-step instructions — through colorful drag-and-drop blocks first, then real typed code. At CodeJam we turn that journey into games, robots, and stories kids are proud to call their own.

Step 1 · Block Coding

Beginners 6–8 snap color-coded blocks together in Scratch Jr — no typing, no reading required. They make characters run, jump, and talk while secretly learning sequencing and logic.

move forward 10
repeat 4 times
play meow sound

Step 2 · Real Code

Ages 11–14 graduate to typed Python — the language pros use! Kids build arcade games, math puzzles, and apps while mastering loops, functions, and algorithms.

quiz_game.py
1for level in range(10):
2  collectStars()
3  beatLevel()

Step 3 · Robotics & AI

Code leaves the screen! Kids 8–14 assemble a Micro:bit robot kit at home, then program it to avoid walls, follow lines, and even recognize poses with beginner AI.

Quick Answers

Top Questions from Parents

Do kids need prior coding experience?

Not at all. Beginner pathways exist for every age — Scratch Jr (6–8), Scratch Arcade (8–10), and Python (11–14).

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

Cohorts never exceed 4 students per coach, so every child gets hands-on code review in every single session.

What happens if we miss a class?

All live sessions are recorded to your student portal, and kids can drop into weekly open mentor office hours.

How does the 30-Day Happiness Guarantee work?

If your child isn't completely delighted after the first 4 classes, we refund 100% of tuition — no questions asked.
