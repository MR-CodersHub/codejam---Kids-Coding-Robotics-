Our Founding Story

Nurturing Tomorrow's Creators, One Joyful Line of Code at a Time

Founded in 2020 by computer scientists and educational psychologists, CodeJam was born from a simple belief: every child can learn computational thinking when code is taught through games, stories, and caring mentorship.

Our Learning Philosophy

How We Make Coding Click for Kids

Play-First Architecture

We don't do dry syntax tests. Kids learn loops by making dance parties, variables by building health bars, and physics by making platformer jumps.

1:4 Cohort Ratios

No giant webinars. Every child codes alongside 3 peers and a dedicated mentor who knows their passions, learning pace, and strengths.

Embrace the Bugs

Debugging is celebrated as a detective adventure. Children learn resilience, emotional regulation, and iterative problem-solving.

COPPA Safety Pledge

Zero advertisements, strictly vetted sandbox environments, and fully transparent parent reporting dashboards.
