Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our live classes, age matching, equipment, and happiness guarantee.

Curriculum & Class Format

What is the student-to-teacher ratio?

Our live cohorts never exceed 4 students per coach. This ensures your child receives hands-on code review, personalized attention, and encouragement every session.

Does my child need prior coding experience?

Not at all! We have beginner entry pathways for every age: Scratch Jr for ages 6–8, Scratch Arcade for ages 8–10, and Python Beginner for ages 11–14.

What happens if we miss a class?

All live sessions are recorded and uploaded to your student portal. In addition, students can attend weekly open mentor office hours to catch up with a live coach.

Equipment & Hardware Kits

What computer equipment is required?

A standard PC, Mac, Chromebook, or iPad with a functioning webcam and microphone. All code editors run smoothly inside the Google Chrome browser.

How is the robotics kit delivered?

Upon enrolling in the Robotics Track, we ship the complete Micro:bit v2 and sensor kit directly to your doorstep via priority courier with tracking.

Tuition & Guarantee

How does the 30-Day Happiness Guarantee work?

If your child isn't completely delighted and excited after the first 4 classes, just let us know and we will promptly refund 100% of your tuition payment.

Do you offer sibling discounts?

Yes! Families enrolling 2 children automatically receive 15% off, and families with 3 or more children receive 25% off their total membership.

Still Have Questions?

Our friendly academic advisors are available 7 days a week to help guide your decision.
