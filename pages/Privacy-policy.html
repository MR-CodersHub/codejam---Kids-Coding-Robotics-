Privacy Policy & Children's Safety Pledge

Last updated: August 2026

1. Our Commitment to Children's Privacy (COPPA)

CodeJam Coding Academy is committed to protecting the privacy of young learners. We strictly comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child data protection frameworks (GDPR-K). We never sell, rent, or monetize any child or student personal data.

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect minimal information necessary to deliver educational programming:

  • Parent/Guardian Information: Name, email address, phone number, and billing details.
  • Student Profile: First name or nickname, age bracket, and code project files saved in the sandbox.
  • Classroom Attendance: Session timestamps and mentor progress feedback.

3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Policy

CodeJam's student portal, interactive game engine, and online code playgrounds are 100% ad-free. No behavioral tracking cookies or third-party marketing trackers are ever loaded in student environments.

4. Data Encryption & Video Classroom Security

All video sessions are hosted on password-protected, encrypted rooms supervised by background-checked educators. Video recordings are accessible solely by the enrolled family and deleted automatically after 90 days.

5. Parental Rights to Inspect or Delete Data

Parents retain full ownership and rights over their child’s records. At any point, a parent may request to inspect, download, or permanently delete all student accounts and project history by contacting our Data Protection Officer at privacy@codejam.academy.
